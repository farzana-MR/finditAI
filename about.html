PROJECT OVERVIEW & ARCHITECTURE

Intelligent Lost & Found Platform

Combining OpenAI CLIP Computer Vision and Sentence Transformers NLP to automate belonging recovery with transparency, mathematical precision, and zero-knowledge privacy.

1. Problem Definition & Mission

In high-traffic environments such as universities, corporate campuses, shopping malls, and public transit terminals, thousands of personal possessions are lost daily. Traditional recovery relies on physical notice boards, manual ledgers, or disjointed social media posts that rarely correlate with found item logs.

FindIt AI solves this with an intelligent multi-modal platform. By transforming photographs into visual embeddings and text descriptions into semantic vectors, our AI matching engine pairs lost reports with found reports in real-time, drastically reducing recovery latency from weeks to minutes.

2. Core AI & Technical Architecture

👁️

Computer Vision (CLIP)

Utilizes OpenAI's Contrastive Language-Image Pre-Training (CLIP ViT-B/32) to project visual features into high-dimensional embedding spaces, capturing geometry, brand styling, color nuances, and form factor.

🧠

NLP (Sentence Transformers)

Encodes detailed text descriptions using all-MiniLM-L6-v2. Synonyms and descriptive variations (e.g., "leather purse" vs "bifold wallet") map to adjacent coordinates in semantic vector space.

📐

Cosine Similarity Metric

Measures the cosine of the angle between normalized vector projections. A score above 0.80 indicates a High Match, while 0.65 to 0.79 triggers a Possible Match review.

🖼️

OpenCV Image Processing

Pre-processes user uploads via OpenCV: background segmentation, color histogram normalization, contour detection, and noise suppression before neural inference.

Multi-Factor Match Formula

To ensure reliable suggestions without false positives, the system calculates an aggregate weighted similarity score across multi-modal dimensions:

Score = (0.40 × CLIP_Visual) + (0.30 × NLP_Semantic) + (0.20 × Location_Score) + (0.10 × Date_Proximity)
≥ 80% Similarity: High Match (Immediate Push Notification)
65% - 79%: Possible Match (Highlighted on Dashboard)
< 65%: Filtered below notification threshold

3. Privacy & Zero-Knowledge Anti-Fraud Verification

To prevent fraudulent claims, FindIt AI strictly adheres to zero-knowledge public displays:

  • Identifying serial numbers, passwords, lock screen wallpapers, and internal wallet contents are concealed from public browsing.
  • A claimant must provide granular, unprompted answers to finder security questions.
  • Authorized administrators or custodial officers verify matching documents before approving in-person handover.
