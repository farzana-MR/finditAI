Intelligent Architecture

Built with Cutting-Edge AI for Unmatched Recovery Rates

Traditional lost and found boards rely on manual browsing. FindIt AI autonomously scans, compares, and notifies users the moment a matching belonging is logged.

👁️

Computer Vision (CLIP + OpenCV)

Extracts deep multi-modal feature vectors from item photographs. Matches items across varying angles, lighting conditions, and partial occlusions.

🧠

Sentence Transformers NLP

Understands context and semantic intent in item descriptions. If one user says "bifold brown purse" and another says "leather Fossil wallet", our NLP model connects them.

🛡️

Anti-Fraud Verification

Private item features and serial numbers are never exposed publicly. Claimants must answer proprietary verification questions before approval.

🏆

Gamified Finder Rewards

Incentivizes honesty and community cooperation. Genuine finders earn reward points, unlock achievement badges, and climb the platform leaderboard.

⚡

Multi-Factor Match Score

Considers 6 critical dimensions: Visual similarity, Text semantics, Item category, Dominant colour, Location coordinates, and Date elapsed.

📱

Instant Multi-Channel Alerts

Receive immediate alerts via in-app notifications and email when our AI detects a high-probability match for your lost report.
